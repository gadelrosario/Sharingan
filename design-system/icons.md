# Icons

## Style

- Simple
- Recognizable
- Consistent stroke weight
- Readable at small sizes

## Rules

- Pair unfamiliar icons with labels.
- Do not use icons as the only signal for destructive actions.
- Keep icon sizes consistent within a component.
- Avoid mixing filled and outlined icon families without purpose.

## Suggested Meanings

- Airplane or navigation: Flight Control
- Eye: Sharingan Vision
- Lightning: Chidori Alert
- Brain or network: Intelligence Core
- Compass or branching path: Best Path
- Clock: Wait or decision timing
- Shield: Roster protection
- Warning triangle: Risk
