# Cards

## Standard Card

- Background: Elevated Surface
- Border: Subtle Border
- Padding: `16px`
- Border radius: `16px`
- Transition: `150ms ease`

## Premium Card

May include:

- Gold accent
- Stronger elevation
- Confidence badge
- Tier badge
- One-line recommendation
- Supporting explanation
- Before-and-after value

## Recommendation Card

Required hierarchy:

1. Recommended action
2. Confidence
3. One-line explanation
4. Supporting evidence
5. Alternate path or wait option

## Player Card

May include:

- Player name
- Position
- Team
- Tier
- Rookie indicator
- TeamFit
- Role or opportunity note

## Interaction

- Hover effects should be subtle.
- Selected cards require a clear border or surface change.
- Avoid large movement animations during live draft use.
- Cards must remain readable without hover.
