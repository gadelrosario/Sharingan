## Usage Rules

- Use gold for premium emphasis, key actions, and important value.
- Use purple for primary navigation and Decision Engine identity.
- Do not use red unless there is urgency, danger, or Sharingan-specific meaning.
- Do not rely on color alone to communicate confidence or status.
- Avoid using more than two accent colors within one component.
