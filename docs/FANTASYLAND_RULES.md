# FantasyLand Strategy Rules

Source basis: principles explicitly enumerated for the previously reviewed FantasyLand transcript. These are summaries, not quotations.

## FantasyLand 1

A Plant Flag is positive conviction, not an automatic selection.

## FantasyLand 2

Conviction may break a close tie between otherwise comparable candidates.

## FantasyLand 3

Conviction may justify only a controlled reach inside an acceptable value window.

## FantasyLand 4

Ceiling and breakout potential gain importance when roster construction can absorb variance.

## FantasyLand 5

Conviction cannot override major tier, price, role, or roster-construction problems.

## FantasyLand 6

Strategic strength and evidence confidence are independent.

## FantasyLand 7

Positive conviction weakens or expires when the supporting role assumption is invalidated.
