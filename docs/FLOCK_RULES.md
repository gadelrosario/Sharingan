# Flock Strategy Rules

Source basis: principles explicitly enumerated for the previously reviewed Flock wide-receiver and running-back transcripts. These are summaries, not quotations.

## Wide Receiver

### Flock WR 101
Bottom-tier projected offenses materially reduce wide-receiver ceiling paths.

### Flock WR 102
Environment affects touchdowns, sustainable drives, yardage ceiling, and weekly consistency.

### Flock WR 103
A poor environment changes acceptable price rather than creating a permanent avoid.

### Flock WR 104
Cheap players in poor offenses can remain viable lottery tickets.

### Flock WR 105
Premium prices in weak offenses require stronger supporting evidence.

### Flock WR 106
Platform-specific ADP can change the quality of the same selection.

### Flock WR 107
Sportsbook expectations are strong evidence inputs, not absolute truth.

### Flock WR 108
Environment confidence and improvement probability moderate penalties.

### Flock WR 109
Talent, role, environment, and acquisition cost remain separate dimensions.

## Running Back

### Flock RB 201
Poor offenses suppress ceiling more strongly than volume-supported floor.

### Flock RB 202
Volume can preserve useful RB2 outcomes in weak offenses.

### Flock RB 203
Bottom-tier offenses reduce top-12 and elite outcome paths.

### Flock RB 204
Receiving work protects floor better than a touchdown-dependent early-down role.

### Flock RB 205
Goal-line and touchdown opportunity should receive an environment penalty.

### Flock RB 206
Repeated inability to overcome a poor environment reduces ceiling confidence.

### Flock RB 207
Unknown elite talent may retain a wider outcome range than repeatedly capped established outcomes.

### Flock RB 208
Offense-improvement probability moderates environment risk.

### Flock RB 209
Coaching, quarterback, line, and scoring-expectation changes can weaken an old environment label.

### Flock RB 210
Acquisition cost determines whether environment risk is acceptable.

### Flock RB 211
League scoring and lineup requirements alter positional value.

### Flock RB 212
Platform-specific ADP changes the running-back value window.
