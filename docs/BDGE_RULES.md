# BDGE Strategy Rules

Source basis: principles explicitly enumerated for the previously reviewed BDGE transcript. These are summaries, not quotations.

## BDGE 1

A price fade is not a permanent player avoid. It remains active only while acquisition cost is outside the acceptable value window.

## BDGE 2

Value windows distinguish poor, fair, good, and excellent acquisition ranges.

## BDGE 3

Recommendation quality must be considered together with acquisition cost.

## BDGE 4

Opportunity cost matters most when premium early-round alternatives remain available.

## BDGE 5

Premium draft capital should purchase access to premium outcomes.

## BDGE 6

Public risk may already be incorporated into ADP and should not automatically be charged twice.

## BDGE 7

Player-quality evaluation and player-price evaluation are separate dimensions.

## BDGE 8

Platform ADP can place the same player in different value windows.
